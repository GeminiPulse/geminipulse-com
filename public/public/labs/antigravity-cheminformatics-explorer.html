Step 1: The Entropic Cost of Rotational Freedom

In solution, a flexible molecule twists across its rotatable bonds into hundreds of non-binding conformations. When locking into an active protein pocket, losing this motion costs thermodynamic entropy (ΔS < 0), weakening net binding free energy (ΔG). Cyclizing into a macrocycle "pays the cost in advance."

from rdkit import Chem

# Target: ALK-resistant G1202R mutation candidate
