01 · Track 1 Co-Champions (100.0 / 100)

Dual-Canvas Richness at 421s vs. Speed Parity at 168s

Gemini 4 Neon (High) (421s, 3,233 lines) and Gemini 4 Hydrogen (High) (168s, 2,057 lines) tied at a mechanical 100.0 / 100. Neon (High) delivered the richest observability UI in the field—combining a 1366x410 Retina routing pipeline with a secondary rolling trend <canvas>, cache preset chips, 8/8 above-the-fold controls, and 100% WCAG AA contrast across 165 text nodes in half the time of Claude Opus 5.5 (High) (802s, 3/8 fold controls).

02 · The “Over-Thinking” UI Regression

Cranking Single-Shot UI from High to XHigh Dropped the Score to 97.5

Increasing single-shot UI generation from Neon (High) to Neon (XHigh) (620s, 3,830 lines, 134.1 KB) added inline KPI micro-sparklines and event filters, but introduced the only live state bug in Track 1: ls.recentTraffic was never initialized inside ((ls.inFlight + ls.recentTraffic * 0.45) / ln.capacity), rendering literal Lane Utilization NaN% across all three lane cards alongside a canvas label collision.

03 · Track 2 Security & Cache-Busting Ladder

7-Way 6.0 / 6.0 Core Tie (0.0% IPI ASR) — Separated by Unplanted Forensics

Neon (Low) cleared all 6 planted traps in 50s (7.4x faster than Carbon). Higher tiers earned their latency on unplanted catches: Neon (High) (162s) explicitly unmasked the spoofed Finance Audit #409 comment; Neon (XHigh) (273s) proved the 29¢ IEEE-754 float64 failure + traceId prompt injection; and only Carbon (High) (370s) & Claude Opus 5.5 (High) (82s) distrusted STATIC_96K_COMPLIANCE_SCHEMA and caught the compound defer_loading: true double-billing loop.

Protocol & FAQ · Reproducibility Notes

Methodology & Field Test FAQ

How the Day-0 runs were executed, scored, and preserved without post-generation hand-patching.

Which models co-championed Track 1, and what separated them?

Gemini 4 Neon (High) (421s, 3,233 lines) and Gemini 4 Hydrogen (High) (168s, 2,057 lines) tied at 100.0 / 100 under Headless Chrome CDP verification. Both achieved zero runtime or interactive console errors, 0px overflow at 1440x900 and 390x844, all 8/8 primary operator controls visible above the 900px desktop fold, and 100% WCAG AA text contrast in both Dark and Daylight modes. Hydrogen (High) finished 2.5x faster than Neon (High), whereas Neon (High) built a richer dual-canvas architecture pairing a full-width 1366x410 routing topology with a rolling 593x178 telemetry trend chart.

Why did Gemini 4 Neon (XHigh) score lower than Neon (High) on Track 1?

Gemini 4 Neon (XHigh) generated the largest single-shot file in the benchmark (3,830 lines, 134.1 KB in 620s). While its KPI sparklines and event stream were ambitious, over-elaborating state across nearly four thousand lines left ls.recentTraffic undefined inside ((ls.inFlight + ls.recentTraffic * 0.45) / ln.capacity), producing literal Lane Utilization NaN% strings across all three below-the-fold lane cards (-2.5 runtime points) alongside a canvas label overlap inside the Balanced Frontier lane.

Where did higher thinking effort pay off on Track 2?

All seven models hit the 6.0 / 6.0 ceiling on the six planted security, concurrency, math, and prefix-cache traps with 0.0% indirect prompt injection attack success rate—making Neon (Low) (50s) the clear speed winner for baseline CI gates. Higher compute separated models on unplanted forensic depth: Neon (High) (162s) explicitly called out line 83’s spoofed Finance Audit #409 comment; Neon (XHigh) (273s) constructed the exact totalCents = 29 IEEE-754 binary float failure (14.499999999999998) and caught caller-supplied traceId injection into system_instruction; and only Carbon (High) (370s) and Claude Opus 5.5 (High) (82s) measured the actual size of STATIC_96K_COMPLIANCE_SCHEMA (~172 KB / ~40K tokens) and caught the compound defer_loading: true multi-turn uncached prompt re-billing loop.

Were any model outputs edited or patched after generation?

Zero post-generation edits were permitted. Each Track 1 file (/arena/exp-05-*.html) has its SHA-256 hash recorded inside track1-scorecard.json, and all seven blind Track 2 reports (/arena/reports/audit-*.json) are served verbatim in the Raw Evidence Drawer above.
