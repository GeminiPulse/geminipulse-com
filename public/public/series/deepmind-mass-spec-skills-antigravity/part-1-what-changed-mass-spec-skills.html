Release Summary: Mass Spec Skills for Google Antigravity (Nov 25, 2026)

Developed by Google DeepMind alongside analytical chemists at the University of Pretoria, Technical University of Denmark (DTU), and Princeton University’s Rabinowitz Lab, Mass Spec Skills equips Google Antigravity to run open-source MS/MS tools (matchms, GNPS, MoNA, SIRIUS, MAGMa, MetFrag, PubChem, and RDKit), boosting 100-compound exact-match accuracy from under 4% to around 97% across Gemini 3.8 Flash, Gemini 4 Argon, and Gemini 4 Carbon while cutting Flash token usage nearly 4x.

1. Solving Mass Spectrometry’s “Dark Matter” Problem

A single sample extract of blood, soil, or plant tissue yields tens of thousands of molecular peaks on a modern tandem mass spectrometer. Because experimental reference libraries only cover a fraction of catalogued chemistry, standard library searches annotate roughly 5% of spectra on average (and about 10% in human samples). Elucidating the remaining unannotated signals requires multi-step formula deduction, isomer ranking, and fragmentation-mechanism verification.

Rather than asking a generalist language model to perform exact-mass arithmetic in prose, DeepMind wrapped established community software into composable Antigravity domain skills so researchers can describe samples and instrument mass tolerances in plain language and receive a verified candidate structure plus an auditable report.
