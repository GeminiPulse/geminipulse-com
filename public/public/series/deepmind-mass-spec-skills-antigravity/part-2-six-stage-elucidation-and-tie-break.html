1. Step-by-Step Walkthrough of Negative-Mode Spectrum m/z 395.1387

Negative-ion tandem spectra are notoriously sparse in public libraries compared to positive-ion datasets. In DeepMind’s end-to-end demonstration (with reference library matching intentionally bypassed after screening), the Antigravity agent executed six deterministic stages from a single natural-language request:

Stage Community Tool / Skill Verified Run Telemetry (m/z 395.1387)
1. Preprocess & Denoise matchms Converts negative precursor [M−H]− at m/z 395.1387 to 396.1459 Da neutral mass and strips 1,742 background spikes (1,761 raw peaks reduced to 19 fragment peaks).
2. Library Lookup GNPS & MoNA Scores denoised peaks by cosine similarity within instrument tolerances; finding no confident match, escalates to de novo formula inference.
3. Formula Deduction SIRIUS Evaluates fragmentation trees across 10 candidate elements, pinning down C20H21ClN6O at −1.5 ppm mass error (score 37.86 vs. 28.33 runner-up).
4. Neutral-Loss Loop MAGMa, MetFrag & Neutral-Loss Lookup Splits precursor into m/z 162.1037 (45.1%) and m/z 232.0283 (6.8%), tracing sequential HCl and HNCO losses via m/z 196.0511 and 189.0238 to 153.0453.
5. Reaction-Path Tie-Break Gemini Chemical Reasoning + PubChem Scores 845 isomers; rules out tied 97.7%-intensity CID 154638626 (no urea, no water-loss peaks) and amide CID 55558151 (bond-break score 49.0) to isolate CID 137835447 (26.0).
6. Valence & Cleavage Check RDKit Confirms valid valences, exact mass match, and renders the 2D molecule highlighting the central asymmetric urea cleavage alongside secondary chlorophenyl-pyrazole peaks (m/z 206.0485, 126.0114).

2. Honest Physical & Database Boundaries

DeepMind notes two clear scientific limits: first, mirror-image molecules (enantiomers) and certain near-identical isomers produce virtually indistinguishable LC-MS/MS spectra and still require retention time, ion mobility, or NMR to separate. Second, genuinely novel compounds absent from PubChem cannot be matched to an existing registry ID, though Antigravity still gives chemists the predicted formula, annotated fragment ions, and partial structural clues.
