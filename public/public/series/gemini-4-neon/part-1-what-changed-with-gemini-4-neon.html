Release Summary: November 18, 2026 (21:00 UTC)

Google DeepMind launched Gemini 4 Neon (gemini-4-neon) in General Availability across the Gemini API paid tier, Google AI Studio, Android Studio, Gemini for business, Google Antigravity (AI Pro & Ultra), and the Gemini app (AI Pro & Ultra). Adapted from Gemini 4 Carbon, Neon is priced at $3.00 per 1M input tokens ($0.15 per 1M cached input tokens, a 95% implicit cache discount) and $15.00 per 1M output tokens, with a unified 1,048,576-token context window, 64K synchronous output / 1M background long decode, four thinking levels (low, medium, default high, xhigh), and immediate enum support in Gen AI SDK v3.6.1 (@google/genai & google-genai).

1. Filling the Sweet Spot in the Gemini 4 Lineup

Following Gemini 4 Carbon (Nov 10) for maximum-horizon reasoning and Gemini 4 Hydrogen (Nov 12) for fast everyday execution, teams still needed a model that delivers frontier-grade depth on complex coding, dense knowledge work, and science without paying Carbon’s thinking-token latency on every high-frequency loop.

As Koray Kavukcuoglu (SVP, Google DeepMind and Chief AI Architect, Google) outlined in the November 18 launch announcement, Gemini 4 Neon pairs a 32% lower cost per task than Carbon at its default high thinking setting with top-three frontier rankings—scoring 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0.1 (xhigh) and 70.9% (#2 overall) on the Vals Index v2.2.
