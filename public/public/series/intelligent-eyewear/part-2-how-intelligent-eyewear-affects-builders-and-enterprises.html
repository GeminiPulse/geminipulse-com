2. Architecture of the Wearable Multimodal Live API Pipeline

Building applications that interface with Google Intelligent Eyewear requires an understanding of the bidirectional streaming loop connecting the glasses, the smartphone companion bridge, and the Gemini Live API:

⚡ Edge-to-Cloud Multimodal Protocol Flow

  1. Audio Ingest: The multi-mic array samples 16kHz or 24kHz single-channel PCM audio, applies on-device beamforming noise cancellation, and streams audio deltas to the companion bridge over Bluetooth LE / Wi-Fi Direct.
  2. Proactive Video / Image Snap: In active conversational mode, the 12MP camera sends JPEG/WebP compressed visual frames (typically 1 to 5 FPS, or triggered on-demand via voice command) directly into the Multimodal Live WebSocket session.
  3. Low-Latency Gemini Processing: gemini-omni-1.2-flash or gemini-4-argon analyzes the interleaved voice and visual tokens in unified context, triggering internal tool calls (Maps routing, Google Workspace, EHR database) or generating instant streaming audio tokens.
  4. Zero-Buffer Audio Return: The Gemini Live API streams raw audio back through the companion app, which routes directly to the custom-tuned open-ear transducers with sub-350ms total conversational round-trip time.

3. Power, Bandwidth & Token Budgeting

Unlike stationary desktop agents or wall-powered server processes, wearable agents live within tight battery (9-hour target) and thermal envelopes. Enterprise architects must design for intelligent token conservation:

  • On-Demand vs. Continuous Video Streaming: Continuously streaming 30 FPS video burns battery in under 90 minutes. Intelligent Eyewear applications should default to continuous audio streaming with event-driven visual keyframes (captured when the user speaks, asks a question, or when significant visual motion deltas are detected).
  • Gemini Decisions API Pre-Filtering: Enterprise workflows can employ Google's new Gemini Decisions API ($0.10/1M input, $0.00 output) on client snapshots to rapidly determine whether full multimodal generation is required, slashing cloud compute costs by up to 80%.
  • Prompt Caching on Repetitive Blueprints: Industrial repair and clinical workflows should cache standard operating procedure manuals and safety rubrics in the Gemini context window, taking advantage of Google's 95% prompt cache discount.

4. Enterprise Workplace Compliance: HIPAA, GDPR & Privacy

Deploying smart glasses into enterprise environments often triggers legal and compliance scrutiny regarding employee surveillance and bystander recording. Google Intelligent Eyewear addresses this directly through architectural guarantees:

🏢 Enterprise Compliance Checklist

  • BAA & HIPAA Readiness: When connected to enterprise Google Cloud Vertex AI and Gemini for Google Workspace tenants with a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA), audio and visual streams are fully HIPAA compliant.
  • Zero-Retention Guarantee: Visual frames evaluated during Live API sessions are processed ephemerally in volatile memory. No user images or voice snippets are stored in history logs or used to train public foundation models.
  • Bystander Face Redaction: Edge-assisted face blurring ensures compliance with European GDPR Article 6 & 9 requirements for public and workplace video processing.
  • Tamper-Proof Hardware Interlock: Facilities managers can verify that employees are not surreptitiously recording sensitive screens; any attempt to tape or obstruct the recording LED disables camera capture at the hardware level.
