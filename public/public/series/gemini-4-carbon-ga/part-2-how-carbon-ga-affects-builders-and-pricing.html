3. Unlocking Long Decode: Past the 64K Output Ceiling

Prior generation models suffered from an artificial 8K or 64K output token ceiling. When generating large-scale migrations, complete Three.js web applications, or comprehensive mathematical proofs, models would abruptly terminate with MAX_TOKENS errors or require fragile chunking harnesses.

Gemini 4 Carbon supports Long Decode: the unified 1,048,576 token window allows a single turn to generate hundreds of thousands of output tokens (up to 1M minus prompt length). A single interaction can now output:

  • A complete, multi-file Three.js WebGL application with custom procedural shaders, geometry buffers, and audio nodes in a single response.
  • An entire formal verification proof in Lean 4 spanning tens of thousands of lines of mathematical theorems.
  • A fully documented, schema-compliant financial audit report spanning 200 extracted engineering tables and balance sheets.
