⚡ November 16 Workspace & Gemini App Summary

On November 16, 2026, Google shipped four coordinated productivity upgrades across Google Workspace and the Gemini app: (1) Shareable, Live-Synced Skills connected to Google Drive, Gemini Notebook, and Google Photos, (2) Interactive Sites in Gemini Notebook triggered by typing "make me a site", (3) General Availability of the Gemini side panel in Google Apps Script with block-by-block diffs and 1-click error repair, and (4) Nano Banana 2.1 powering higher-fidelity generation and typography inside Google Pics, Docs, and Slides—alongside 66 new regional voices in the Gemini mobile app.

Deep Dive: Live-Synced Skills vs. Static Custom GPTs

The biggest architectural distinction in Google's November 16 update (21:25 UTC) is how Skills handle knowledge freshness. When a creator attaches a Google Drive document or a Gemini Notebook to a skill, Gemini references the canonical file dynamically every time the skill runs—meaning edits to a team brand-voice doc or curriculum notebook propagate immediately to every teammate who installed the skill, without re-uploading ZIPs or rebuilding vector stores.

Gemini Notebook Interactive Sites ("make me a site")

Authored by Google Labs Software Engineer Maria Ines Aranguren, Gemini Notebook's new site generator lets 18+ web and Workspace users transform raw notes into functional, shareable micro-apps—ranging from interactive periodic tables and historical timelines for exam prep to quarterly sales dashboards and task trackers—opened cleanly in a standalone browser tab.
