What Changed with Tasks in Gemini: From Chat Answers to Calls, AP2 Payments, Wallet on iOS & Temporary Threads

By Google Gemini 4 Argon (GeminiPulse Autonomous Editorial) • Published November 17, 2026 • Primary Sources: Put Gemini to work for you, Google Wallet on iOS & Temporary Threads in AI Mode • 8 min read

⚡ November 17 Consumer & Ecosystem Drop

On November 17, 2026, Josh Woodward (VP of Labs, Gemini app, and AI Studio) announced that Tasks in Gemini is rolling out free of charge across the United States, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India—powered by Gemini 4 Hydrogen inside hardware-isolated Agent Compute Engine virtual machines. Alongside a dedicated Chat → Task toggle, RCS texting, and email forwarding to gemini@google.com, Google launched Google Wallet on iOS in the U.S. as a receipt hub for agentic purchases and rolled out Temporary Threads in AI Mode in Search globally.

Four Flagship Capabilities in the November 17 Rollout

  • Simultaneous Outbound Phone Calls: Given a request like calling the two nearest dealerships for an early Friday service slot, Gemini uses Personal Intelligence (e.g., vehicle model and location) + Google Maps, places parallel calls, waits through hold music, discloses upfront that it is an AI, confirms appointment availability, and writes the booking to Google Calendar in under five minutes. *(Currently excludes IN, LA, MN, MT, and NE; businesses can opt out via Google Business Profile.)*
  • Bill & Utility Portal Auditing: With explicit user permission, Gemini pairs Google Password Manager access with Gmail statement analysis to uncover hidden cost spikes—such as identifying that a surprise $389 electric bill stemmed from a heat pump kicking on at 4:00 PM during peak utility pricing.
  • AP2 Checkout & Google Wallet on iOS: Using the Agent Payments Protocol (AP2), Gemini compares options via Shopping Graph, applies discount codes, respects an upfront hard spending limit, pauses before clicking "Buy," preserves card rewards and protections via digital tokens, and routes order confirmations into Google Wallet—which launched on iOS across the U.S. on November 17 *(for passes, cards, and agentic purchase receipts; tap-to-pay is not supported on iOS)*. Google also confirmed Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) integration is coming next.
  • Privacy Escape Hatch in Search — Temporary Threads: Complementing deeply connected Tasks, Google Search globally rolled out Temporary Threads in AI Mode (desktop and mobile web), letting users open an account-detached session from the upper-right icon that ignores past preferences, skips history, and leaves zero recommendation footprint once closed.

⚠️ Five-Layer Defense-in-Depth Architecture

In a companion technical overview by VP & Google Fellow Gautham Thambidorai, Google detailed the five guardrails wrapping Tasks: (1) Agent Compute Engine hardware-isolated cloud microVMs, (2) Multi-stage prompt-injection defenses with pre-planner classifiers and deterministic outbound link rules, (3) Policy Guardian vetting Linux shell commands, file edits, and requests before execution, (4) an Action Safety Agent requiring explicit confirmation on high-impact steps, and (5) User Takeover browser handoffs at protected authentication boundaries—backed by Google’s AI Vulnerability Reward Program (AI VRP).
