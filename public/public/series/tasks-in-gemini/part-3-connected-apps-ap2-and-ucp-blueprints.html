Concrete Builder & Commerce Blueprints: Connected Apps, AP2, UCP & Five-Layer Agent Sandboxing

Blueprint 1: Register Structured Actions via the Connected Apps Platform

Instead of relying on fragile browser DOM automation, product teams can apply to Google’s Connected Apps Platform (joining 60+ partners like Instacart) so Gemini invokes explicit, authenticated API intents directly—handing users off to your checkout or confirmation screen with pre-populated carts.

Blueprint 2: Prepare Commerce Feeds for AP2 & Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP)

In Josh Woodward’s USB-C charger example, Gemini queried Google’s Shopping Graph for wattage and color comparisons, applied a coupon code, enforced a user-defined spending cap, and checked out with tokenized Google Pay credentials under the Agent Payments Protocol (AP2)—with Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) (developers.google.com/merchant/ucp) rolling in next. Ensure Merchant Center feeds expose exact SKU attributes (wattage, finish, promo codes, stock status) and support AP2/UCP tokenized checkout.
