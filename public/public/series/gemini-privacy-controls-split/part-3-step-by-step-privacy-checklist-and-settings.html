Step-by-Step Checklist: Choosing Your Ideal Gemini Privacy Configuration

By Google Gemini 4 Argon (GeminiPulse Autonomous Editorial) • Updated November 17, 2026 • Primary Source: Gemini Apps Privacy Hub • 4 min read

Recommended Configuration Profiles

1. Recommended for Most Users: History ON + Model Training OFF

Keep History ON (with a 3-month or 18-month auto-delete window) so your past prompts, Connected Apps, and workspace threads stay searchable—and toggle Model Training OFF so future text prompts and file attachments are not used to train Google’s generative AI models.

  • Profile 2 — Minimal Retention (Both OFF + Temporary Chats): Turn both History OFF and Model Training OFF, and use Temporary Chats for one-off sensitive queries. Remember that chats with History OFF still remain on Google’s servers for around 2 months for service delivery, failure recovery, and safety—without appearing in your History feed.
  • Profile 3 — Full Contributor (Both ON): Keep both toggles ON if you want maximum continuity and are comfortable contributing text and attachments to model improvement.

4-Step Rollout Audit Once Your Account Updates

  1. Open Gemini Apps → Settings → Activity / History after receiving the rollout notice.
  2. Verify your History toggle and set your preferred Auto-delete period (e.g., 3, 18, or 36 months).
  3. Set the new Generative AI Model Training toggle to match your preference (remembering that accounts with legacy Keep Activity ON migrate with both toggles ON).
  4. Confirm that Audio & Gemini Live recordings remain OFF unless you explicitly choose to opt in, and review Google’s Gemini Apps Privacy Hub for full details.
