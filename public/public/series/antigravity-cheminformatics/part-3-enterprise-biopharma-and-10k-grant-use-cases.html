3. The Responsible Boundaries of Computational Chemistry

DeepMind researchers emphasize an essential scientific reality: computational designs are testable hypotheses, not finished medicines.

Assay noise, inter-laboratory variance, and narrow dynamic measurement ranges limit the predictive precision of any machine learning model. What Antigravity delivers is not a replacement for experimental chemistry, but a massive acceleration of the hypothesis generation, curation, and retrosynthetic planning loop—allowing medicinal chemists to spend their time synthesizing high-probability leads rather than wrestling with spreadsheets and file formats.
