# Google Suite Everyday `(T)winning` Workflows

Use this skill when helping non-technical users, founders, marketers, or small-business teams get immediate time savings inside **Google Workspace with Gemini** and **Google NotebookLM**.

## 5 High-ROI Daily Recipes

1. **Gmail — Thread Triage & Decision Table:**
   > *"Summarize this email thread into 3 sections: (1) Decisions already agreed on, (2) Open questions waiting on me, and (3) Draft a 4-sentence reply confirming timelines."*
2. **Google Docs — Proposal & SOW Refiner (`@File` Grounding):**
   > *"Reference @[Client Discovery Notes] and draft a 1-page executive summary with scope milestones, deliverables table, and explicit out-of-scope assumptions."*
3. **Google Sheets — Formula Coach & Categorizer:**
   > *"Write an `XLOOKUP` + `ARRAYFORMULA` that matches Column B SKUs against the `Pricing` tab without breaking when new rows are added, and explain what each argument does in plain English."*
4. **Google Slides — Visual Executive Deck Outline:**
   > *"Turn @[Q4 Strategy Doc] into an 8-slide narrative arc: Hook, Market Shift, Customer Pain, Our Solution, ROI Math, Proof, Roadmap, and Next Steps—with 3 crisp bullets max per slide."*
5. **Google NotebookLM — Zero-Hallucination Audio & Research Briefing:**
   - Upload up to 50 PDFs, Google Docs, or YouTube transcripts into `https://notebooklm.google`, generate an **Interactive Audio Overview** to listen on your commute, and ask source-pinned questions where every claim links directly to the exact paragraph in your uploaded docs.
