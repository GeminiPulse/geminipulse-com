## 2. Planned 4-Part Blog Article Series Blueprint (`The Antigravity Design Evolution Series`)

When we publish the public blog series on **GeminiPulse.com**, it will be structured as a 4-part visual case study with live clickable iframe/link embeds to all 10 preserved prototypes:

### Part 1: *Killing the 24,000px AI Dashboard — Why We Froze Our v2.0 Monolith and Built Three Divergent Worlds (`Era 0 → Rounds 1A, 1B & 1C`)*
- **Focus:** The anatomy of cognitive overload (`Pill Soup`, `Box-in-Box Nesting`, `185-word teaser cards`) and why incremental CSS tweaks can never fix an exhausting information architecture.
- **Visual Exhibits:** Side-by-side comparison of `Era 0` (`24,043px`) vs. `Look 1A` (Risograph Editorial), `Look 1B` (Spatial Gallery), and `Look 1C` (Guerrilla Studio).

### Part 2: *Not Three Themes, Two Fraternal Twins — Synthesizing $\alpha$ Castor & $\beta$ Pollux and Surviving the Mobile/WCAG Critic (`Rounds 2D, 3D & 4E`)*
- **Focus:** How the astronomical duality of **Gemini (`♊`)** unified editorial journalism (`α Castor`) and interactive tooling (`β Pollux`), plus a transparent look at how an automated `ui-craft-critic` audit caught our `309px` mobile header bloat and how we rescued the composite score from `6.4/10` to `9.4/10`.
- **Visual Exhibits:** `Look D v1` vs. `Look D v2` mobile/desktop diffs + `Look E`’s pinned `(0,0)` Ultramarine Continuity Bridge.

### Part 3: *When Inspiration Gets Too Close — The `thefutureisyesterday.com` Collage Experiment (`Look G → Look H`)*
- **Focus:** The tension between functional clarity (`Look G`) and unforgettable visual soul (`Look H`). Shows how we fused classical marble/clockwork intaglio engraving (`look-h-twin-oracle.webp`), warm newsprint, and a solid `#181816` Nocturnal Workbench into `GEMINI in two MINDS.`—and why Neil made the call to **save Look H as a time capsule** rather than let GeminiPulse look like the visual twin of his other site, `thefutureisyesterday.com`.
- **Visual Exhibits:** Full-page renders of `Look G` (`shot-look-g-1440x900.webp`) and `Look H` (`shot-look-h-1440x900.webp`, `look-h-twin-oracle.webp`).

### Part 4: *The Constellation IS the Navigation — Where Silicon Valley Meets French Couture (`Look I → Look J v6`)*
- **Focus:** How a single creative prompt from Neil (*"keep the inspiration, like a modern magazine that Silicon Valley to French fashion would find alluring... and maybe the hero IS the Gemini Constellation illustration and clicking on stars takes you places"*) produced **Look I** and evolved through six 3D precision passes into **Look J v6 (Round 14 — Masterpiece Edition)**.
- **Deep Dive:**
  - Designing the **Velvet Midnight Obsidian (`#050811`) + Deep Sapphire Night (`#07162C → #0C2342`) + Deep Amber-Bronze Night (`#211104 → #2E1907`) + Ethereal Digital Purple (`#A385FF`)** palette and `Cormorant Garamond` typography.
  - Art-directing the panoramic copperplate engraving (`look-i-celestial-twins.webp`) so classical marble Castor and haute-horlogerie clockwork Pollux gaze across an open celestial corridor with subtle outer-left Sapphire and outer-right Golden-Amber sky horizon glows.
  - Plotting the real IAU **Gemini (`♊`)** constellation (`α Castor`, `ε Mebsuta`, `μ Tejat`, `η Propus + M35` on the left chain; `β Pollux`, `δ Wasat`, `ζ Mekbuda`, `γ Alhena` on the right chain) in true 3D light-year depth (`34 LY` to `1,200 LY`), with full `360°` rotational freedom, deterministic seeded starfield particles (`0x47454D49`), Quintic S-Curve Ramp slewing, `0.55px` watchmaker hairlines, a **Zero-Collision 2D Candidate Slot Solver** (`0 px²` overlap across all 8 stars via `svgEl.getScreenCTM()`), the **`GEMIN♊PULSE` Twin-Pillar SVG Wordmark**, 100% bespoke 4-chapter full-viewport (`100svh`) articles with a docked bottom **Constellation Bar Active Table of Contents**, and our flagship **Open Letter to Google DeepMind** (`/series/google-preview-observatory/open-letter-to-google-deepmind.html`).
- **Visual Exhibits:** `look-i-celestial-twins.webp`, `shot-look-i-1440x900.webp`, `shot-look-j-1440x900.webp`, `shot-look-j-drawer-open.webp`, and `shot-look-j-3d-orbit.webp`.
